Autopost

Prepare media and publish it to the right channel, on schedule.

Autopost is a locally operated tool maintained by dipnaked for publishing the operator’s own photos and videos. It prepares Instagram posts and uses YouTube API Services to upload and schedule videos on explicitly connected channels.

This is an information page for a personal integration. The publishing tool runs on the operator’s computer; this website does not offer public registration or collect channel credentials.

What it does

  1. Prepare the media

    Process selected local files, adjust media formats and audio, transcribe a video, and prepare its title, description, tags and thumbnail.

  2. Publish to a chosen account

    Run the Instagram publishing workflow or upload a video to the YouTube channel selected during authorization. YouTube uploads start as private videos.

  3. Keep a publication schedule

    Assign a future release time on YouTube and check the saved video status. Local publication records let an interrupted run continue without uploading the same video again.

YouTube access

The operator signs in directly with Google and grants access to a selected YouTube channel. Autopost uses that access to identify the channel, read its video and scheduling information, upload videos, and update titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and publication times.

The operator controls the source files, connected channels and publishing configuration. Prepared text and thumbnails can be reviewed before upload, and scheduled videos can be managed in YouTube Studio.

Data and privacy

Authorization tokens and publication records are stored on the operator’s computer. Video preparation includes cloud generation through OpenAI: selected local video frames and transcript text are sent to prepare publishing assets.

Read the Autopost privacy policy for the data used, third-party processing, storage and access-revocation details.

Terms of use

Autopost is intended for personal use by its operator. Connect only accounts and channels you are authorized to manage, and publish only material you have the right to use.

By using Autopost’s YouTube integration, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Review the privacy policy before authorizing the integration. Publication settings and generated assets remain the operator’s responsibility.
