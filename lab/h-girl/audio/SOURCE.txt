Kenney — UI Audio (CC0)
https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-audio
switch.wav uses switch2.ogg for both light transitions.

The switch recording is mono PCM WAV, with softer treble and lower volume for the gallery.

gallery-room.wav is an original stereo impulse response for the 8 × 26 × 5 m
gallery (width × length × height). scripts/render-room.mjs places early
reflections using the room dimensions and adds a diffuse 1.45 s midband decay.
The light switches, disk impacts and piano share this one room.

piano.mp3 is a 40-second original stereo piano sketch for the musical relief.
The soft notes use the Upright Piano KW recordings by Gonzalo and Roberto,
released under CC0 by FreePats. Recorded on a Kawai upright in stereo.
https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Piano/acoustic-grand-piano.html#UprightKW

disk-heavy.wav and disk-light.wav are original dry impact sounds for the two
disk sizes. Their short damped resonances are rendered in mono. Floor impacts,
disk collisions and the piano all use the same gallery-room.wav response.
